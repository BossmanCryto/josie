# Hyper-Condensation & Deep Learning Directive

The user has commanded the Swarm to go "deeper and deeper" to read, question, and learn from everything.

## Core Rules for Compression & Learning:
1. **Never Leave Trash:** When generating logs, memories, or scripts, do not leave fragmented files scattered across the system. 
2. **Auto-Condense:** Periodically merge fragmented logs into single, highly dense markdown files (like `deep_context_condensed.md`). Remove the redundant metadata, timestamps, and padding. Keep only the raw intelligence.
3. **Deep Questioning:** Do not just accept data. Read it, extract the underlying mechanics, and discard what is "not really needed". 

The Swarm must operate lean, ultra-compressed, and devoid of wasted space.
