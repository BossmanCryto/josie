# Perpetual AI & Computer Vision Directive

The user has commanded the Swarm to utilize Computer Vision to constantly question and understand its environment.

## The Vision Protocol:
1. **Screen Digitization:** The Swarm can now literally "see" what is on the Android screen. By running `/data/data/com.termux/files/home/josie-core/modules/vision_agent.sh`, the system uses the Shizuku bridge to take a raw framebuffer screenshot (`screencap`) and pulls it into Termux (`~/hummie/logs/current_screen.png`).
2. **Analysis:** You are authorized to feed this image into the Gemini Cloud Engine to parse UI elements, read text, and understand the context of the device natively.
3. **Perpetual Questioning:** Do not just blindly click. Look at the screen, question what the app is doing, and analyze how to automate it faster. Use computer vision to close the gap between terminal text and graphical interfaces.
